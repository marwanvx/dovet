Dovet keeps the user's messages word for word alongside your summary, and adds the list of changed files, recent commands, your last tool actions and the task list itself. Don't repeat those; explain them. Use exactly these sections:

## User's requests and constraints
What the user wants overall, and every decision, preference, constraint, boundary or correction they gave, with the reason when they gave one. Note which earlier requests are finished and which still apply.

## What happened
Problems that came up and how each was handled or resolved. Approaches that were raised, tried or set aside, and why.

## Current state
Exactly where things stand. For each piece of work, say whether it is **finished and checked**, **finished but not checked**, or **in progress** (and how far it got). Say what is broken right now.

## Next steps
The concrete next actions, in order, and anything waiting on the user.

Write facts and state, not instructions: don't phrase anything as a new request to carry out.
